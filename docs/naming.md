# Naming

Why the names are what they are. For new readers.

## hekatus

**Hekate + ultrasound.**

In the myth of Persephone's abduction, Hekate (Ἑκάτη) is the goddess who
**alone heard the cry**, testified to what she had heard, and **carried
torches into the dark to search**. Ultrasound diagnosis listens for the echo
of a transmitted pulse and makes the unseen interior of the body visible: the
one who hears, and the one who brings light. Hekate holds both in one figure.

The tail of the name is for ultrasound (US) — and lands, by a fortunate
accident, on *Hekatos* (Ἕκατος), an attested epithet of Apollo: **"the one
who works from afar and strikes the mark at will."** Scholars read Hekate's
own name as its feminine form. Pulse-echo imaging works from afar; a
beamformer strikes any chosen point at will; and Apollo is, besides, a god of
healing.

Hekate is also the goddess of crossroads and boundaries, standing where three
roads meet and seeing three ways at once.

## The three roles

| | Origin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **enodia** | Ἐνοδία | *On the road.* An old epithet of Hekate — the divinity that dwells in the road itself |
| **diaplous** | διάπλους | *A crossing; a passage across.* Not an epithet of Hekate — **hardware independence, stated as a name.** The back end carries data across from acoustic geometry (scanline × depth) to the seen image |
| **lampas** | λαμπάς | *Torch.* Raise it, and the surroundings become visible. The *dadouchos* (torch-bearer) minus the bearer: only the instrument remains |

enodia walks the deterministic real-time path; diaplous makes the crossing to
the visible world; lampas burns beside them, observing without touching.

(Strictly, enodia → diaplous is a series path with lampas running alongside,
not a three-way fork — but read as one figure with three aspects, the image
holds.)

## Ecosystem

| | Origin | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **sitos** | σῖτος | *Grain.* A distributed parameter store |
| **sitometron** | σιτομέτρης | *The one who measures and rations grain.* Admission, scheduling, supervision, and observation of the process fleet |

Raw channel data arrives directly over Ethernet — the river that turns the
water-wheel, not cargo to be carted. What `sitos` carries is not the grain
but **the instructions for milling it**: delay tables, filter coefficients,
contribution maps.
